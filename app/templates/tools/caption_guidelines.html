How To Get Viral Captions

Follow these simple rules to maximize engagement, reach, and performance.

1. Be Specific

Instead of writing: "Politics in Nigeria"
Write: "Tinubu signs new tax reform bill"

Or either paste a prompt or use our snippet in our news page for the news you want.

2. Choose the Right Tone

  • Neutral: Informative and balanced.
  • Breaking: Urgent and direct updates.
  • Viral: Short, punchy, scroll-stopping captions.
  • Emotional: Human-centered and feeling-driven.
  • Professional: Structured and authoritative.

3. Understand the Modes

  • Single: One high-impact caption.
  • Captions: Three high-impact caption.
  • Thread: Multi-tweet storytelling format.
  • Reply: Designed to spark discussions.
  • Engagement: Curiosity-driven content.
  • Ultra Viral: Explosive captions designed to dominate X.

4. Write Like This

Weak: The government has announced a new reform.

Better: A new reform just passed.

Ultra Viral: Power just shifted.
