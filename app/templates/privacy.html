Privacy Policy – Superior News

Effective Date: February 4, 2026

At Superior News, your privacy and trust are our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit or post on our public blogging platform, Superior News.

By using our website and services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Superior News, we may collect the following types of information:

a. Personal Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, and any other details you provide when signing up.
  • Content you post, including articles, comments, images, or any submissions.
  • Feedback or messages you send to us.

b. Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages you visit, time spent on the site, and browsing behavior.
  • Cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for purposes such as:

  • Operating, maintaining, and improving Superior News.
  • Allowing you to post, comment, and interact with other users.
  • Responding to your inquiries, feedback, or support requests.
  • Sending updates, announcements, and promotional content (if you opt-in).
  • Analyzing trends and improving the functionality and user experience of our platform.

3. How We Share Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information. However, we may share information:

  • With service providers who help us operate our website (e.g., hosting, analytics, or email services).
  • To comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, or protect the safety of our users.
  • In the event of a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences.
  • Enhance your browsing experience.
  • Analyze website traffic and performance.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

5. Your Privacy Choices

  • Editing or Deleting Posts: You can edit or remove any post you’ve made.
  • Email Preferences: You can unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us at contact@superiornews.app.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While we strive to keep your information safe, no online platform can guarantee 100% security.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Updated policies will be posted here with a revised Effective Date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: contact@superiornews.app
  • Website: www.superiornews.app

Your privacy matters. At Superior News, we are committed to keeping your information safe while letting you share your voice with the world.
